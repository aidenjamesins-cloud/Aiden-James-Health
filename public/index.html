How pricing works

Two ways a health plan sets your price.

Marketplace plans price everyone together. Private PPO plans price you on your own health. If you’re healthy, that difference is the whole conversation.

Marketplace

Priced as a pool

Your health history doesn’t change your price. Age, area and tobacco use do. Pre-existing conditions are always covered, and tax credits depend on income.

Private PPO

Priced on you

Medically underwritten, so your own health history sets the price along with age and state. Healthy people can pay less, and you can apply any month of the year.

$63,840

Under current rules, Marketplace tax credits stop at 400% of the poverty line. For one person on a 2027 plan, that’s $63,840 a year. Earn more and you pay the full Marketplace price, which is where a private PPO often costs less.
