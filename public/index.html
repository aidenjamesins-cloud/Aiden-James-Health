Questions, answered

Don’t see yours? Call or text (813) 451-0821.

Why use an advisor instead of shopping on my own?

I can see options across carriers, including private plans that don’t appear on the Marketplace, and narrow them down to the few that actually fit. You get the full picture without the hours of research.

What if I have a pre-existing condition?

Tell me upfront. Marketplace plans can’t turn you down or charge more for pre-existing conditions. Some private plans ask health questions, so I’ll match you with plans that fit your history.

Can I keep my doctors?

Often, yes. Many PPO plans let you see doctors nationwide without referrals, and I confirm your doctors and hospitals are in network before you apply.

Do I have to wait for open enrollment?

Not always. Many private plans accept applications year-round, and life events like losing coverage, moving, or having a baby can open a special enrollment window for Marketplace plans.

What does it cost to work with you?

Nothing. My advice and quotes are free. I’m paid by the insurance carrier when you enroll, not by you.

What happens after I enroll?

I stay your point of contact. Claims, billing, and renewal questions come straight to me.
