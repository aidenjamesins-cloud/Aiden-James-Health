Does it cost anything to work with you?

No. Carriers pay me when you enroll, and your premium is the same either way. The review is free whether or not you change anything.

Do I have to wait for Open Enrollment?

Not for private plans. You can apply any time of year. For Marketplace plans, 2027 Open Enrollment runs November 1 to January 15, and a life event like losing job coverage, moving or having a baby opens a 60-day window.

What if I have a pre-existing condition?

Tell me up front. Marketplace plans always cover pre-existing conditions. Private plans may decline you or exclude a condition, so I’ll point you to what fits.

Can I keep my doctors?

Often, yes. I check your doctors and hospitals against each plan’s network before you apply.

Are private PPO plans cheaper than Marketplace plans?

Sometimes. If you’re healthy and don’t qualify for tax credits, a private plan can cost less. If you qualify for credits or have ongoing conditions, the Marketplace often wins. The review shows you both numbers.

Is a private PPO the same as major medical insurance?

Not always. Some private plans are comprehensive major medical. Others are limited-benefit plans that pay set amounts toward care. I’ll tell you exactly which one you’re looking at.

I’m leaving my job. Is COBRA my only option?

No. COBRA keeps your current plan, but you usually pay the full premium plus up to 2%. Losing job coverage also opens a 60-day Marketplace window, and private plans accept applications year-round. I’ll compare all three before your deadline.

What if I’m already well covered?

I’ll tell you, and that’s it. No follow-up sales calls.

What happens after I enroll?

I stay your point of contact. Claims, billing and renewal questions come straight to me.

Can you help my business offer coverage?

Yes. I help small businesses compare and set up group health plans, and I can show you individual options for your team too.
